Privacy Policy

What this app does

RYMEC Campus Navigator helps students and visitors find campus locations and request navigation routes.

Location information

If you choose “Locate me”, your browser may provide your device location to this page so a route can be calculated. Location access is controlled by your browser and is not required to browse the campus directory.

Saved preferences

Favorites and locally edited demo locations may be stored in your browser's local storage. They are not automatically shared with the college server.

Analytics

If you accept the analytics notice, the site enables Vercel Web Analytics to measure website usage. You can clear the site's local storage to remove the local consent preference.

External services

Maps and routing may use third-party services such as OpenStreetMap, Esri and OSRM. Their own terms and privacy practices apply when those services are requested.

Contact

For corrections or campus-location updates, contact the project administrator/college through the official channels.

Use of the navigator

This application is provided as a campus information and navigation aid. Users should follow campus signage, staff instructions and safety rules.

Location accuracy

Map markers, building positions and routes may be approximate. Confirm the destination on site when necessary.

Third-party maps

Map tiles and routing are supplied by external services and may change availability, coverage or terms.

Content corrections

Campus administrators may update location information. Users should report incorrect or outdated information through the appropriate college channel.

Availability

The service may be unavailable during maintenance or because of network, map-provider or hosting issues.
